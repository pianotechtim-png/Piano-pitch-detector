How to record ▶
Play the 8 A keys one at a time, lowest to highest. Takes about 30 seconds.
  1. Hold each note 2–3 seconds and let it ring — don't rush.
  2. Leave a short silence between notes.
  3. Do not use the pedals.
  4. Each A is the white key between the 2nd and 3rd black keys of the group of three black keys (see diagram).
  5. Phone audio or video is fine. Record in a quiet room.
