How to record ▶
Play the 5 A keys from A2 to A6, one at a time, lowest to highest. Takes about 20 seconds.
  1. A2 is the 3rd A from the bottom of the keyboard. Play it, then each A going up — five in total.
  2. Hold each note 2–3 seconds and let it ring — don't rush.
  3. Leave a short silence between notes.
  4. Do not use the pedals.
  5. Each A is the white key between the 2nd and 3rd black keys of the group of three black keys (see diagram).
  6. Phone audio or video is fine. Record in a quiet room.
  7. Playing all 8 A's (A0–A7) also works — the extra notes are shown for reference only.
