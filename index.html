How to record ▶
A piano's A keys are numbered A0 through A7 — A0 is the very lowest key on the piano. This test uses the five middle A's, A2 – A6 (purple below):
  1. Tap Start listening and allow microphone access.
  2. The app asks for one note at a time — play the A it shows and hold it 2–3 seconds until it's captured.
  3. Do not use the pedals.
  4. Record in a quiet room, phone within a few feet of the piano.
  5. Each A is the white key between the 2nd and 3rd black keys of the group of three black keys:
Can't record live? Use the upload option at the bottom — record audio or video of A2–A6 played lowest to highest with short silences between notes.
